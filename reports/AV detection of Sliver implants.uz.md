# Skanerlar ochilgan implantni kuzatadi

Antivirus va EDR mahsulotlari Sliver C2 klientini bitta hash yoki bitta «yomon API» sifatida aniqlamaydi. Ular **olti mustaqil qatlam** ni yig‘adi: diskdagi oila YARA, generic Go PE-ML, decrypt dan keyingi **xotira YARA**, executable allocation lar ustida kernel **ETW-Ti**, behavioral C2/injection ML, va default HTTP/TLS **tarmoq barmoq izlari**. Generate formati (`exe`, `shared`, Donut `shellcode`) skanerlar dam holatda ko‘radigan **o‘ram** ni va birinchi mapping ning **xotira turini** o‘zgartiradi: OS yuklagan EXE yoki DLL fayl-backed **`MEM_IMAGE`**; `VirtualAlloc` dan Donut PIC unbacked **`MEM_PRIVATE`** — Donut o‘z mapperi ishlaguncha, va o‘sha mapper ham decoy `SEC_IMAGE` yo‘li bo‘lmasa pagefile-backed view qoldiradi ([Payload Compatibility](https://sliver.sh/docs?name=Payload+Compatibility); [TheWover/donut](https://github.com/TheWover/donut); [MEMORY_BASIC_INFORMATION](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winnt/ns-winnt-memory_basic_information)). Unpack dan keyin EXE, SHARED va shellcode **bir xil ~16–30 MB Go image** ni yugurtiradi, shuning uchun **stock teamserver da `sliverpb` va protobuf RPC nomlari qoladi** — garble ularni o‘zgartirmaydi ([Immersive Labs](https://www.immersivelabs.com/resources/c7-blog/detecting-and-decrypting-sliver-c2-a-threat-hunters-guide); [BishopFox/sliver#1760](https://github.com/BishopFox/sliver/issues/1760)). Unique SHA-256 va wrap XOR aniq-hash IOC va ba’zi dam holatdagi blob satrlarni uradi; jarayon xotirasidagi o‘sha nomlarni qayta yozmaydi, Microsoft xotira-skaner dvigateli esa **cloud analogiga ega emas** ([Advanced technologies of MDAV](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/adv-tech-of-mdav)). Hujjatlangan Windows **tetiklar** call-stack provenance va remote-inject ketma-ketliklari: Unbacked freymdan `winhttp`/`wininet`/`ws2_32` ning `LoadLibraryExW` si, Unbacked dan Winsock `connect`, va post-ex da `VirtualAllocEx` → `WriteProcessMemory` → `VirtualProtectEx` → `CreateRemoteThread`. Go IAT dagi `VirtualAlloc` yoki `LoadLibraryExW` ning o‘zi tetik emas ([Fortbridge: Reforging Sliver](https://fortbridge.co.uk/research/reforging-sliver-how-simple-code-edits-can-outmaneuver-edr/); [Network Module Loaded from Suspicious Unbacked Memory](https://github.com/elastic/protections-artifacts/blob/main/behavior/rules/windows/defense_evasion_network_module_loaded_from_suspicious_unbacked_memory.toml); [Looking for the ‘Sliver’ lining](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2022/08/24/looking-for-the-sliver-lining-hunting-for-emerging-command-and-control-frameworks/)). BishopFox AV evasion ni **doira tashqarisida** deb belgilaydi ([Anti-virus Evasion](https://github.com/BishopFox/sliver/blob/master/docs/sliver-docs/pages/docs/md/Anti-virus%20Evasion.md)).

## Stock EXE, SHARED va Donut turli static skanerlarga tushadi

Diskda format **o‘ram sinfi**, boshqa implant emas. Microsoft default payload larni shellcode, executable, shared library/DLL va service deb belgilaydi; operatorlar default PE ni tashlab shellcode ni custom loader ga joylashtiradi, chunki **fayl-asosidagi** aniqlash birinchi sirt — ichki image o‘zgarmagani uchun emas ([Looking for the ‘Sliver’ lining](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2022/08/24/looking-for-the-sliver-lining-hunting-for-emerging-command-and-control-frameworks/)). Stock `exe` — katta imzosiz Go PE (Immersive o‘rtacha **~16 MB**, Fortbridge **30 MB gacha**) haqiqiy IAT, `gopclntab` va ochiq protobuf bilan. `shared` — xuddi shu c-shared Go image plus **export directory**: CLI default `StartW,VoidFunc,DllInstall,DllRegisterServer,DllUnregisterServer` (`StartW` rundll32 uchun, `VoidFunc` PowerSploit `Invoke-ReflectivePEInjection` uchun, COM nomlari `regsvr32` uchun) ([BishopFox/sliver#1782](https://github.com/BishopFox/sliver/issues/1782); [v1.2.1 sliver.go](https://github.com/bishopfox/sliver/blob/v1.2.1/sliver/sliver.go)). Windows `generate --format shellcode` **PIE EXE** ni kompilyatsiya qiladi va Donut bilan konvert qiladi; BallisKit 2025 izohi to‘g‘ri: «raw shellcode baribir **PE ustidagi o‘ram**» ([binaries.go](https://github.com/BishopFox/sliver/blob/master/server/generate/binaries.go); [BallisKit](https://blog.balliskit.com/tutorial-sliver-c2-with-balliskit-macropack-and-shellcodepack-452e94c24b19)).

Ommaviy **fayl YARA** PE magic dan ko‘ra implant ASCII da kelishadi. Elastic `Multi_Trojan_Sliver` uchta qoida, hammasi `scan_context = "file, memory"`: protobuf getter lar (`).RequestResend`, `).GetPrivInfo`), Go package path `B/Z-github.com/bishopfox/sliver/protobuf/sliverpbb` plus RPC tiplari (`InvokeSpawnDllReq`, `NetstatReq`, `HTTPSessionInit`, `ScreenshotReq`, `RegistryReadReq`), va uchinchi qoida **session-start/register bytecode** — satr emas ([Multi_Trojan_Sliver.yar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elastic/protections-artifacts/main/yara/rules/Multi_Trojan_Sliver.yar)). Immersive fayl qoidasi PE/ELF/Mach-O magic **va** `sliverpb` **va** `github.com/bishopfox/` **va** `chacha20poly1305` ni talab qiladi; NCSC SVR advisory `/sliver/`, `sliverpb.` va sakkizta `*Req` nomini kalitlaydi; ditekSHen/Malpedia va Wazuh xuddi shu oilani qayta ishlatadi; Chronicle GCTI protobuf nomlari o‘rniga C2 URL-scheme CMP immediate larini moslashtiradi ([sliver.yar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Immersive-Labs-Sec/SliverC2-Forensics/main/Rules/sliver.yar); [NCSC gist](https://gist.github.com/ecapuano/2c59ff1ea354f1aae905d6e12dc8e25b); [Wazuh](https://wazuh.com/blog/detecting-sliver-c2-framework-with-wazuh/); [ditekSHen INDICATOR_TOOL_Sliver](https://github.com/ditekshen/detection/blob/e76c93dcdedff04076380ffc60ea54e45b313635/yara/indicator_tools.yar); [GCTI Sliver_Implant_64bit](https://github.com/chronicle/GCTI/blob/main/YARA/Sliver/Sliver__Implant_64bit.yara)). Elastic nashr etgan Sliver YARA **`StartW`, `VoidFunc` yoki ` Go buildinf:` ni ro‘yxatlamaydi**. Bular Fortbridge ning [roadwy/DefenderYara](https://github.com/roadwy/DefenderYara) dan olgan extract i: to‘qqizta ASCII igna `DllUnregisterServer`, `DllRegisterServer`, `DllInstall`, `GetJitter`, `VoidFunc`, `GetKill`, `AddTunnel`, `GetIsIOC` va ` Go buildinf:` (boshidagi bo‘shliq). Fortbridge **ishlatilmagan export larni olib tashlagan** va **`StartW` ni alohida nomlagan**; `StartW` o‘sha to‘qqiz satrli ro‘yxatda yo‘q edi. Splunk ESCU «Suspicious Rundll32 StartW» — Cobalt Strike uchun yozilgan **process-creation** hunt, Sliver `rundll32 implant.dll,StartW` ni ham ushlaydi ([Reforging Sliver](https://fortbridge.co.uk/research/reforging-sliver-how-simple-code-edits-can-outmaneuver-edr/); [Splunk ESCU](https://research.splunk.com/endpoint/9319dda5-73f2-4d43-a85a-67ce961bddb7/)). Microsoft inbox nomlari `Trojan:Win64/Sliver.D`, `ALF:Trojan:Win32/Sliver.A`, `VirTool:Win64/Splinter.A` va `Behavior:Win32/Splinter.A` oila yorliqlari sifatida mavjud; Microsoft ularning baytlarini **e’lon qilmaydi** ([WDSI Trojan:Win64/Sliver.D](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/wdsi/threats/malware-encyclopedia-description?Name=Trojan:Win64/Sliver.D)).

Donut `.bin` bitta static sinfni boshqasiga almashtiradi. Default Donut **Bypass=3 (continue on fail)** AMSI/WLDP/ETW patch ni **loader stub** ga kompilyatsiya qiladi, ixtiyoriy shifrlangan modul tashqarisida. Operatorlar stock Sliver generate-shellcode ga MDAV **`Win32/AMSI_Patch_T.B13`**, Donut PIC ning `execute-shellcode` siga **`Behavior:Win32/AMSI_PATCH_T.B12`** ni bog‘lagan — jumladan **`-b 1` (none)** ishlatilgan va C# Donut chiqishida baribir B12 olgan hisobot ([sliver#1814](https://github.com/BishopFox/sliver/issues/1814); [sliver#2200](https://github.com/BishopFox/sliver/issues/2200); [donut#165](https://github.com/TheWover/donut/issues/165)). Entropy=3 fayldagi ochiq `sliverpb` ni decrypt gacha yashirishi mumkin; AMSI stub qoladi. SGN qo‘llansa, **RWX talab qiladigan o‘zini o‘zi o‘zgartiruvchi decoder** ([sliver#1410](https://github.com/BishopFox/sliver/issues/1410)). Stock EXE/SHARED da o‘sha Donut stub **yo‘q**.

Ikkinchi static qatlam **Sliver-spetsifik emas**. `Trojan:Win32/Wacatac.B!ml` — Microsoft PE-ML (`!ml` = klassifikator). Formidable uni **zararsiz** imzosiz Wails/Go GUI da, **nol** sandbox dynamic aniqlash bilan urgan; muallif tashxisi Go PE shakli — katta `.text`, **`gopclntab`**, `runtime.morestack_noctxt`, statik bog‘langan, CRT yo‘q — Sliver ham kiradigan Go-malware hajmi o‘qitgan ([Formidable](https://formidable.tools/posts/defender-wacatac-go-binary/)). Praetorian `-trimpath -ldflags "-s -w"` aniqlashni **oshirganini**, ML ning **`gopclntab` ga og‘irlik berishini**, va kattaroq «ghost profile» Microsoft Wacatac ni **~95% dan ~20%** ga tushirganini o‘lchagan; cloud ML lokal `MpCmdRun` dan agressivroq, modellar taxminan **kunlik** qayta o‘qitiladi ([Praetorian: Adversarial Oracles](https://www.praetorian.com/blog/llm-edr-signature-reduction/)). ## Unique hash va wrap XOR callback da tugaydi

Microsoft gibrid stack birinchi ko‘rilgan imzosiz PEni **xususiyat, emulyatsiya, detonation va behavior** bilan baholaydi; SHA-256 **kesh kaliti**, verdict emas. Cloud metadata PE atributlari, ImpHash, imzolovchi, MotW, yuklab olish URL, **API chaqiriqlari** va **process injection** ni o‘z ichiga oladi. Block at First Sight Internet-zona noma’lumni default **10 soniya** (60 gacha) lock qiladi, cloud detonatsiya qilayotganda; `.exe`/`.dll` default submission siyosatida «safe samples» ([Cloud protection and sample submission](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/cloud-protection-microsoft-antivirus-sample-submission); [Block at first sight](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/configure-block-at-first-sight-microsoft-defender-antivirus)). SmartScreen imzosiz yangi hash uchun fayl-hash reputation **noldan** boshlanadi; «noma’lum» «ishonchli» emas, EV sertifikatlar endi darhol reputation bermaydi ([SmartScreen reputation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/package-and-deploy/smartscreen-reputation)). Embed qilingan blob ning XOR i ichki satrlarni **dam holatda** yashiradi — emulator yoki jonli jarayon decrypt qilguncha; emulator «emulyatsiya paytidagi behavior ni va **emulyatsiya oxiridagi xotira mazmunini** skanlaydi» ([Advanced technologies of MDAV](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/adv-tech-of-mdav)). gatari 2024 labi empirik ajratma: XOR **diskdagi** imzolarni urdi; `VirtualAlloc(PAGE_EXECUTE_READWRITE)` + copy + RWX hududning **to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri CALL** i keyin **runtime da** o‘ldirildi, keyingi VirusTotal yuklash esa **butun fayl hashini** known-bad qildi — yangi VDM offset emas, cloud tahlil ([A Trip Down Memory Lane](https://gatari.dev/posts/memory-lane/)).

Implant ishga tushgach, skan ostidagi obyekt **jarayon manzil maydoni**. Microsoft maxsus **client** xotira-skaner dvigatelini hujjatlaydi («ishlayotgan jarayon ishlatadigan xotira maydonini skanlab, kod obfuscation orqali yashiringan zararli behavior ni ochadi»), cloud ustuni **N/A** ([Advanced technologies of MDAV](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/adv-tech-of-mdav)). Immersive xotira aniqlash osonligining bir qatorli sababi: **packing yoki staged yetkazish qanday bo‘lmasin, butun Go binary xotiraga ochilishi shart**. Ularning **xotira** qoidasi so‘zma-so‘z `sliverpb`, chunki fayl qoidasining magic/offset lari dump da yiqiladi ([Immersive Labs](https://www.immersivelabs.com/resources/c7-blog/detecting-and-decrypting-sliver-c2-a-threat-hunters-guide)). Elastic ning uchta Sliver qoidasi `file, memory`. Fortbridge disk satrlari tozalangandan keyin ham `bishopfox` da **faqat-runtime** YARA hit oldi — format satrni RAM da yashirmadi. Donut default header overwrite DOS/PE headerlarini nollaydi yoki almashtiradi; **native PE ning kod va ma’lumoti qoladi** ([Donut README](https://github.com/TheWover/donut)). Fortbridge sinagan ommaviy daraxtida Sliver **sleep mask** yo‘q; idle beacon lar ochiq Go/protobuf ni resident tutadi ([Reforging Sliver](https://fortbridge.co.uk/research/reforging-sliver-how-simple-code-edits-can-outmaneuver-edr/)).

Birinchi callback **o‘ram PE ishga tushishiga ruxsat** berilganini isbotlaydi, implantning ko‘rinmasligini emas. Unique wrap hash, XOR-at-rest, skip-`CreateThread`, hide-console va qisqa Sleep/RAM emulator darvozalari **pre-jump / hash-IOC** nazoratlari. Ular decrypt qilingan imagedagi `ScreenshotReq` ni qayta yozmaydi. Client behavioral blocking keyin **process tree** larni cloud ML ga yuboradi va **ishlayotgan** artifact ni o‘ldirishi mumkin; MITRE-taktika nomlari `Behavior:Win32/CommandAndControl.*!ml` ni o‘z ichiga oladi. Event **1015** maxsus «suspicious behavior» hodisasi; **1116/1117** umumiy malware detected / action-taken va fayl, AMSI, NIS yoki real-time dan ham yonadi — o‘zlari behavioral aniqlashni isbotlamaydi ([Client behavioral blocking](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/client-behavioral-blocking); [Defender event IDs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/troubleshoot-microsoft-defender-antivirus)). Inbox MDAV (`MsMpEng`) buni MDE litsenziyasisiz qiladi; MDE portal alert va EDR-in-block-mode qo‘shadi (default o‘chiq) ([Microsoft Defender Antivirus in Windows](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/microsoft-defender-antivirus-windows)).

`interactive` yangi implant generate qilmaydi. Rasmiy tutorial: u **shu beacon jarayonida session ulanish loop ini yugurtiruvchi goroutine** ochadi. Beacon versus session **tarmoq ritmi** (sleep versus long-poll), PID emas va ikkinchi Go image emas ([Beacons vs Sessions](https://sliver.sh/tutorials/?name=2+-+Beacons+vs+Sessions)). Microsoft 2022 hunt lari **in-memory config** ni chiqaradi, chunki implant check-in uchun uni decrypt qilishi shart — keyingi inject dan mustaqil, implant jarayonining o‘zidagi xotira-skan qatlami ([Looking for the ‘Sliver’ lining](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2022/08/24/looking-for-the-sliver-lining-hunting-for-emerging-command-and-control-frameworks/)).

## Disk LoadLibrary MEM_IMAGE qiladi; VirtualAlloc PIC Unbacked qoladi

Microsoft 2017 reflective-DLL modeli Stephen Fewer ning loader blob ini imzolamagan. U yuqori-xavfli jarayonlardagi **g‘ayritabiiy executable `VirtualAlloc`/`VirtualProtect`** ni baholagan, jumladan PE-ga o‘xshash kontentli katta RWX hududlar va **backing modul yo‘q**. Bu sinf hali Elastic «Unbacked» call stack lari va MDE injection alert lari sifatida tirik ([Detecting reflective DLL loading](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2017/11/13/detecting-reflective-dll-loading-with-windows-defender-atp/); [Doubling Down](https://www.elastic.co/security-labs/doubling-down-etw-callstacks)). Sliver **implant** Windows shellcode — Donut, sRDI emas (`sideload` hujjatlari hali sRDI deydi; 2023 manba izi `DonutShellcodeFromPE` deb atagan). `--format shared` generate qilib diskdan `LoadLibrary` qilish 2017 modeliga **tushmaydi** (Windows loader, `MEM_IMAGE`, modul ro‘yxati to‘ladi). `--format shellcode` generate qilib `VirtualAlloc`+CALL **tushadi**.

Fortbridge 2025 ketma-ketligi ommaviy A/B. Protobuf rename Elastic **satr** YARA ni tushirdi; `GetMaxConnectionErrors()` ni inline qilish bytecode qoidasi `3d6b7cd3` ni tushirdi; Donut Bypass **3→1** AMSI-stub sinfini tushirdi. Stock Sliver ni packer, self-inject va hollowing Elastic va Defender ga **samarasiz** chiqdi. HTTP beacon init hali Go `windows.NewLazyDLL` → PIC dan `ws2_32`/`wininet` ning **`LoadLibraryExW`** sini ishlatgan. Elastic **«Network Library Loaded from Unbacked Memory»** alert bergan. Ommaviy artifact sarlavhasi **Network Module Loaded from Suspicious Unbacked Memory** (id `aa265fbd-…`): `ws2_32.dll` / `wininet.dll` / `winhttp.dll` library load, `call_stack_contains_unbacked == true` va summary `Unbacked` bilan tugaydigan qisqa ro‘yxatdan biri (odatiy `ntdll.dll|kernelbase.dll|Unbacked`). Harakat **`kill_process`**. Qardosh qoida, **Network Connect API from Unbacked Memory** (min endpoint 8.14.0), Winsock `connect` ni `call_stack_final_user_module.name == "Unbacked"` bilan kalitlaydi va ham `kill_process`. Module stomping yiqildi, chunki **16–30 MB** Go implantga realistik qurbon `.text` yo‘q; Donut decoy (`-j`) xuddi shu hajm pastki chegarasiga ega (`SEC_IMAGE` decoy ≥ `SizeOfImage` bo‘lishi shart). DLLlarni oldindan yuklash yiqildi, chunki `NewLazyDLL()` baribir `LoadLibraryExW` chaqiradi. Hardware-breakpoint hook lar alert ni tozalamadi ([Reforging Sliver](https://fortbridge.co.uk/research/reforging-sliver-how-simple-code-edits-can-outmaneuver-edr/); [network-module toml](https://github.com/elastic/protections-artifacts/blob/main/behavior/rules/windows/defense_evasion_network_module_loaded_from_suspicious_unbacked_memory.toml); [connect toml](https://github.com/elastic/protections-artifacts/blob/main/behavior/rules/windows/command_and_control_network_connect_api_from_unbacked_memory.toml); [Almond](https://offsec.almond.consulting/evading-elastic-callstack-signatures.html)).

Elastic Agent li hostda callback bergan ish **boshqa Win32 nom emas**. Bu **implant baytlarini kim map qilishi**. **SHARED** implantni diskka yozib `LoadLibrary`/`GetProcAddress`/`CreateThread` export orqali image mapper (`NtMapViewOfSection` / `LdrLoadDll` **image section** bilan) dan o‘tadi va **`MEM_IMAGE`** beradi. Keyingi `winhttp.dll` ning `LoadLibraryExW` sida stack da `sliver.dll` (image) bo‘ladi, `Unbacked` emas. `VirtualAlloc` hech qachon `MEM_IMAGE` yaratmaydi. Decoy siz Donut `NtCreateSection(..., SEC_COMMIT, PAGE_EXECUTE_READWRITE)` ishlatadi — pagefile-backed, Elastic uchun hali unbacked. Hasherezade kontrast i xuddi shu Windows fakti: qo‘lda map qilingan PElar **`MEM_PRIVATE`** ga tushadi; qonuniy loader mapping **`MEM_IMAGE`** ([Donut inmem_pe.c](https://github.com/TheWover/donut/blob/master/loader/inmem_pe.c); [hasherezade](http://hasherezade.github.io/)). Disk SHARED to‘qqiz ASCII satr va `bishopfox` qayta nomlanguncha **static** Defender YARA ga duch kelgan. Fortbridge jonli Microsoft cloud-ML «rename dan keyin 0 detections» skrinshotini **e’lon qilmaydi**; o‘sha qog‘ozdagi Defender dalili extract qilingan YARA plus LitterBox. «Nol aniqlash» ni parafraz qiluvchi agregatorlar mustaqil lab qo‘shmaydi.

`CreateProcess` bilan ishga tushgan EXE ham `MEM_IMAGE`. Fortbridge DLL ni tanlagan, chunki packer hikoyasi «shu papkadan kutubxona yuklash», EXE o‘z-o‘zidan issiqroq bo‘lgani uchun emas. 2022–2026 da boshqa jihatdan bir xil Sliver build larining EXE versus DLL versus Donut `.bin` uchun ommaviy yonma-yon MDAV/Elastic aniqlash-darajasi jadvali **yo‘q**. Keltirilgan yozuvlardan **behavioral** shovqinni tartiblang: Donut unbacked PIC (AMSI stub, RWX/SGN, unbacked network-module load) > remote CRT inject (`sideload`/`spawndll`/`migrate`) > OS ishga tushirgan disk EXE/DLL. **Static** shovqin: disk EXE/DLL (ayniqsa default COM/`VoidFunc` export li SHARED) > xom `.bin` (MZ yo‘q; Bypass≠1 bo‘lsa Donut stub). Unpack dan keyin **xotira YARA (`sliverpb`) formatlarni tartiblamaydi**.

## Hujjatlangan API tetiklar IAT emas, provenance

Uchta sensor sinfini alohida tuting. **IAT** — diskdagi PE import directory dagi nomlar — PE-ML uchun fayl xususiyati, runtime hunt emas. **ETW-Ti** (`Microsoft-Windows-Threat-Intelligence`) — `ntoskrnl` dan `NtAllocateVirtualMemory` / `NtProtectVirtualMemory` / `NtMapViewOfSection` / `NtWriteVirtualMemory` ning kernel logi (`EtwTiLogAllocExecVm`, `EtwTiLogProtectExecVm`); user-mode unhooking uni olib tashlamaydi; obunachilar PPL-Antimalware bo‘lishi shart. Elastic 8.11+ o‘sha hodisalarni call-stack behavior lar bilan boyitadi (`unbacked_rwx`, `direct_syscall`, `sensitive_api`, `allocate_shellcode`, `execute_fluctuation`) ([Doubling Down](https://www.elastic.co/security-labs/doubling-down-etw-callstacks)). **Xotira skan** o‘sha APIlar allaqachon ishlagandan keyin jarayon VA da YARA moslashtiradi, baytlar qanday kelganidan mustaqil.

`CreateThread` ni tashlash ALLOCVM/PROTECTVM ni **tashlamaydi**. Bu hodisalar executable sahifalar yaratilganda yoki himoya o‘zgarganda yonadi. PIC ga to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri CALL/JMP baribir o‘sha sahifalarni executable qilishi kerak. Elastic 8.11 ETW-Ti API ro‘yxatida **`CreateThread` yo‘q**; `VirtualAlloc`/`VirtualProtect`/`MapViewOfFile` va `Ex` variantlari, plus `QueueUserAPC`, `SetThreadContext`, `WriteProcessMemory` bor. gatari empirik: Defender `VirtualAlloc(RWX)` + `RtlMoveMemory` + **`CreateThread` siz function-pointer CALL** ni baribir flag qilgan ([A Trip Down Memory Lane](https://gatari.dev/posts/memory-lane/)). Bir `VirtualAlloc` da RWX ajratish executable himoyali ALLOCVM beradi (`PAGE_EXECUTE_READWRITE` = `0x40`). Avval RW, keyin `VirtualProtect` RX — PROTECTVM (chaaqiruvchiga qarab Elastic `execute_fluctuation` / `sensitive_api`). Ikkalasi ham thread API siz ko‘rinadi ([Memory Protection Constants](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/memory/memory-protection-constants)).

Native Go EXE/PIC **AMSI ga tushmaydi**. AMSI opt-in host interfeys (`AmsiScanBuffer`). Microsoft qo‘llab-quvvatlagan hostlar PowerShell, JScript, VBScript, WSH, **.NET Framework 4.8+ (assembly lar)**, WMI va Office JS/VBA/XLM Microsoft 365 Apps bilan — Go emas ([AMSI on MDAV](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/amsi-on-mdav)). `execute-assembly` **AMSI ga kiradi**, chunki CLR ni yuklaydi, u in-memory `Assembly.Load` ni skanlaydi. Elastic Sliver `execute-assembly` ni 8.8 da unbacked RWX dan `clr.dll` yuklashning ishchi misoli qilgan (`call_stack_summary : "*mscoreei.dll|Unbacked*"`) ([Upping the Ante](https://www.elastic.co/security-labs/upping-the-ante-detecting-in-memory-threats-with-kernel-call-stacks)). `amsi.dll` ni patch qilish **boshqa** aniqlash (Elastic **AMSI Bypass via Unbacked Memory** / **AMSI or WLDP Bypass via Memory Patching**; Defender `AMSI_Patch_T.*`) va sof Go HTTP beacon uchun shart emas.

Microsoft 2022 hunting yozuvi **default post-ex buyruqlar** ni (`migrate`, `sideload`, `spawndll`, `execute-assembly`, `getsystem`, `psexec`, `shell`) yuqori-signal sirt deb oladi, **`VirtualAllocEx` → `WriteProcessMemory` → `VirtualProtectEx` → `CreateRemoteThread`** bilan amalga oshirilgan. MDE misol alert: **«A process was injected with potentially malicious code.»** GetSystem: `SeDebugPrivilege` keyin **`spoolsv.exe`** ga `CreateRemoteThread`. Sideload/SpawnDll/Execute-Assembly: **`notepad.exe` ni 10 soniya ichida** yaratish va inject. PsExec: `%WINDIR%\Temp\<10 alnum>.exe`, DisplayName **`Sliver`**, Description **`Sliver implant`**. Shell: aniq command line `powershell.exe -NoExit -Command [Console]::OutputEncoding=[Text.UTF8Encoding]::UTF8` ([Looking for the ‘Sliver’ lining](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2022/08/24/looking-for-the-sliver-lining-hunting-for-emerging-command-and-control-frameworks/); [SigmaHQ](https://github.com/SigmaHQ/sigma/blob/master/rules/windows/process_creation/proc_creation_win_hktl_sliver_c2_execution_pattern.yml); [VMware TAU](https://blogs.vmware.com/security/2023/01/detection-of-lateral-movement-with-the-sliver-c2-framework.html)). Hunt & Hackett token API lari `OpenProcessToken` / `ImpersonateLoggedOnUser` / `DuplicateTokenEx` ni qo‘shadi ([Hunting for a Sliver](https://www.huntandhackett.com/blog/hunting-for-a-sliver)). Joriy `task_windows.go` hali `rwxPages` false bo‘lmasa `VirtualAlloc` RWX ishlatadi, remote inject hali CRT to‘rtligini ishlatadi ([task_windows.go](https://github.com/BishopFox/sliver/blob/master/implant/sliver/taskrunner/task_windows.go)). Bu ketma-ketlik disk-run EXE ning idle HTTP poll loop i **emas**.

## Default HTTP va CRT post-ex formatdan mustaqil qoladi

Default HTTP procedural C2 — **tarmoq / URI / header** detektori, API detektori emas. Rasmiy mapping: **`.woff` stager lar**, **`.js` long poll**, **`.html` key exchange**, **`.php` session**, **`.png` close**; yo‘ldagi extension dan tashqari hamma narsa e’tiborsiz. Encoder har xabar uchun **nonce query parameter** orqali tanlanadi; Base32/58/64 alifbolari o‘zgartirilgan; English-words encoder 1 420 qattiq kodlangan so‘z ishlatadi ([HTTPS C2](https://sliver.sh/docs/?name=HTTPS+C2); [Immersive Labs](https://www.immersivelabs.com/resources/c7-blog/detecting-and-decrypting-sliver-c2-a-threat-hunters-guide)). Immersive Snort hali o‘sha extension lar plus cookie lar `PHPSESSID|SID|SSID|APISID|csrf-state|AWSALBCORS` va path prefikslarini (`/php/`, `/api/`, `/js/`, `/static/`, …) moslashtiradi. Proofpoint ETPRO 2021-oktabrda `Sliver Framework HTTP C2 sessionInit` deb nomlagan. NetWitness 2025 hali **Windows executable magic qaytaradigan `.woff`** ni default stager deb hujjatlaydi. cyber.wtf Suricata 2024 HTTP POST dagi English-word encoder tokenlarini ovlaydi. Immersive aniq yozadi: **bu path va extension larning hammasi sozlanadi**. `http-c2.json` ni o‘zgartirish URI barmoq izini yengadi; unbacked xotiradan `LoadLibraryExW`, ETW-Ti ALLOCVM yoki protobuf ustida xotira YARA ni yengmaydi ([sliver.snort](https://github.com/Immersive-Labs-Sec/SliverC2-Forensics/blob/main/Rules/sliver.snort); [Proofpoint](https://www.proofpoint.com/us/blog/security-briefs/ta551-uses-sliver-red-team-tool-new-activity); [NetWitness](https://community.netwitness.com/s/article/689643); [cyber.wtf](https://cyber.wtf/2024/12/06/detection-of-c2-using-suricata/)).

TLS barmoq izlari **server-tomon va versiya-ajratilgan**. Nashr etilgan JARM hashlar allaqachon kelishmaydi: pat_h/to/file 2021 mTLS `28d28d28d00028d00043d28d28d43d47390d982d099a542ccbc90628951062`; cedowens/Nuclei Go 1.15 `2ad2ad0002ad2ad00041d2ad2ad41da5207249a18099be84ef3c8811adc883`; Cybereason 2023 HTTPS `3fd21b20d00000021c43d21b21b43d41226dd5dfc615dd4a96265559485910` (va ikkinchi all-zero prefix hash), **mTLS uchun ishlamasligi** aytilgan. Nuclei ning 2021-davr hash i 2026 da ham shablonlarda va yangi stack larni o‘tkazib yuboradi. Implant uchun keng keltirilgan **JA3 client** hash topilmadi. Ovchilar o‘rniga **cert `CN=multiplayer` / issuer `O=operators`** (Shodan `product:sliver`, Red Canary 2025 BYOVM), TCP **31337** (multiplayer/gRPC), TCP **8888** (default mTLS), UDP **51820** (WireGuard) ishlatadi. Cybereason: **443-portdagi** aloqa aniqlash omili bo‘lish uchun juda keng tarqalgan ([Hunting Sliver](https://blog.tofile.dev/2021/09/04/sliver.html); [nuclei sliver-c2-jarm](https://github.com/projectdiscovery/nuclei-templates/blob/main/network/jarm/c2/sliver-c2-jarm.yaml); [LevelBlue/Cybereason](https://www.levelblue.com/blogs/spiderlabs-blog/sliver-c2-leveraged-by-many-threat-actors); [Beyond the bomb](https://www.zscaler.com/blogs/cybersecurity-best-practices/email-bombing-virtual-machine)). Palo Alto ATP 2025-iyun **Threat ID 89961** «Evasive Sliver C2 Traffic Detection» ni TLS 1.3 yozuvlari ustida **decrypt siz** sequence neural net orqali da’vo qiladi. Vectra 2026-iyul: encoder rotation oddiy beacon clustering ni buzadigan ko‘p-tasma hajmlar beradi; ularning modellari hali Sliver-uslubidagi timing/yo‘nalishni nishonga oladi ([ATP June 2025](https://docs.paloaltonetworks.com/content/techdocs/en_US/advanced-threat-prevention/new-features/by-date/advanced-threat-prevention/june-2025.html); [Vectra](https://www.vectra.ai/blog/detecting-sliver-c2-when-advanced-beaconing-tries-to-hide-in-plain-sight)). Hunter’s Ledger 2026 bitta mTLS kampaniya uchun Suricata ni tashlagan, chunki kanal «ushlangan JARM/JA3S siz oddiy TLS dan farqlanmas» ([Hunter’s Ledger](https://the-hunters-ledger.com/hunting-detections/sliver-open-directory-detections/)).

Microsoft 2022 hunt lari idle implantning default HTTP/mTLS callback ini **kalitlamaydi**. Ular **post-ex API ketma-ketliklari va default config lar** ni kalitlaydi. Tarmoq-tomon default-HTTP tutishlar uchinchi-tomon IDS (ETPRO, Immersive Snort, Corelight, PAN ATP) plus MDAV ning e’lon qilinmagan network/behavior ML. Yovvoyi jarayon nomlari Sliver generate qilgan ikki-so‘zli nomlar (`WILD_PRIDE`, `nasty_roast`) yoki operator tanlagan (`1HTTPS.EXE`), qattiq `sliver.exe` emas ([The DFIR Report](https://thedfirreport.com/2024/08/12/threat-actors-toolkit-leveraging-sliver-poshc2-batch-scripts/)). Fortbridge string-clean + disk `LoadLibrary` dan keyingi ochiqligi: **o‘rnatilgan buyruqlar hali Elastic behavioral alert larini tetiklagan**. BallisKit 1.7.3 implantni ShellcodePack bilan o‘rab, sinovdagi (nomsiz) EDRlarda stageless EXE buyruq bajarilishini va **ikkinchi stage xotiraga yuklanganda tutib qolingan EXE stager lar** ni xabar qilgan — stage-2 yana ochilgan implant ([BallisKit](https://blog.balliskit.com/tutorial-sliver-c2-with-balliskit-macropack-and-shellcodepack-452e94c24b19)).

## Xulosa

Sliver ustida detection engineering **har qatlamga qaysi obyektni ko‘rishga ruxsat** ekanligiga yig‘iladi. Dam holatda obyekt o‘ram: EXE/DLL da Go-PE ML va oila YARA, `.bin` da Donut AMSI-stub YARA, SHARED da export-table ASCII. Birinchi ishga tushishdan keyin obyekt **shu PID dagi ochiq Go implant**, va unique hash / XOR / skip-CreateThread / qisqa Sleep allaqachon sarflangan. Satr o‘rniga **hujjatlangan 2024–2026 EDR qoidasini** o‘zgartiradigan yagona format tanlovi mapping primitiv — disk image-section `LoadLibrary` (`MEM_IMAGE`) versus `VirtualAlloc` PIC (`Unbacked`) — va u ham client xotira skaneri ko‘radigan view da `sliverpb` ni qoldiradi. Protobuf RPC identifikatorlari **klient/server shartnomasi**: teamserver stock ekan, ular implantda qoladi — shuning uchun garble Elastic `Multi_Trojan_Sliver` ni yopmagan va Fortbridge ishlagan yo‘l aqlliroq o‘ram emas, **fork qilingan schema**.
